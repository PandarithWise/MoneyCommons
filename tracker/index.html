SAVING & DEBT METHODS

Simple methods worth knowing

No magic formula. These are practical frameworks you can understand, compare and adapt.

❄️

Debt snowball

Pay minimums on every debt, then put your extra money toward the smallest balance. When it is cleared, roll that payment into the next debt.

Best for: motivation and visible progress.
🏔️

Debt avalanche

Pay minimums on every debt, then direct extra money toward the debt with the highest APR. This can reduce interest costs.

Best for: focusing on the maths.
💷

Pay yourself first

Choose a realistic savings amount and move it aside when income arrives, rather than waiting to see what is left at the end of the month.

Best for: building a consistent saving habit.
🛟

Build a buffer

Before aggressive extra payments, consider a small emergency buffer that can stop an unexpected bill pushing you back into debt.

Best for: reducing financial shocks.
Inflation-aware goal

Money usually buys less over time. Use an assumption to see what a future goal might cost rather than treating today's price as fixed.
